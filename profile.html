AD1DA — Doug Alencar | Williamson County, Texas

Vanity call AD1DA, previously KJ5NZA. I also hold PU8ABD in Brazil — because apparently one country's worth of callsigns wasn't enough.

I'm an Extra-class ham based in Williamson County, Texas, with interests that span analog voice, digital modes, and emergency communications. By day I work in cybersecurity and DevSecOps, which keeps the overlap between radio systems and information security feeling pretty natural — turns out "threat modeling" and "antenna modeling" rhyme for a reason.

Station

  • HF: Icom IC-7300
  • VHF/UHF Base: Icom IC-9700
  • Mobile: Icom IC-2730A
  • HTs: BTECH UV-PRO, Baofeng DM-32UV, Baofeng BF-F8HP Pro

Antennas are an ongoing creative exercise — my lot has HOA restrictions, so stealth designs are the name of the game. Think less "tower," more "ninja."

GMRS

I'm also active on GMRS under WSLC458 with Baofeng GM-15 Pro HTs, primarily for family communications — because nothing says "dinner's ready" like a properly keyed-up radio.

Digital Modes

I'm actively working with D-STAR, AllStarLink, Winlink, packet radio, APRS, and FT8. The digital side of the hobby is where I spend most of my time right now, whether that's gateway configuration, terminal mode experiments, chasing weak-signal DX on FT8, or exploring what's possible with position reporting and messaging.

Nets & Community

You'll find me on the WCARC N5TT repeater system, especially the Monday Night New Hams Net. I check in with WC-ARES on the Sunday Night Training Net and am part of the WC-ARES Digital Cavalry program. I also enjoy the East Coast Reflector — the Morning Brew and Open Mic nets are a regular part of my week.

Thanks for the QSO — and remember, 73 means "best regards," not "73 unread packets."

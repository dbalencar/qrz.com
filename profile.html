AD1DA is operated by Doug Alencar, located in Williamson County, Texas.

I am interested in HF, VHF/UHF voice, local repeater operation, and practical communications. I value clear, disciplined operating practices and the community aspect of well-run local and regional nets.

I am also interested in APRS, including position reporting, messaging, and situational awareness use cases. In addition, I have a strong interest in SKYWARN, ARES, and RACES, with a focus on emergency communications, public service, and supporting local response efforts. I am actively learning best practices related to emergency and field operations.

By profession, I work in the technology and cybersecurity field, which aligns naturally with my interest in radio systems, RF fundamentals, communications reliability, and operational preparedness.
